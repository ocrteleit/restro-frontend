## 🎨 **Color Psychology for Restaurants**

### **🟠 Orange (Recommended Primary)**
- **Psychology:** Appetite, energy, warmth, friendliness
- **Effect:** Makes customers hungry, creates urgency
- **Best for:** Main brand color, call-to-action buttons
- **Examples:** McDonald's, Amazon, Fanta

### **🟢 Green**
- **Psychology:** Freshness, health, nature, growth
- **Effect:** Suggests healthy, fresh ingredients
- **Best for:** Success messages, healthy options
- **Examples:** Starbucks, Whole Foods, Subway

### **🔴 Red**
- **Psychology:** Urgency, passion, appetite, energy
- **Effect:** Creates urgency, stimulates appetite
- **Best for:** Important actions, delete buttons, alerts
- **Examples:** Coca-Cola, KFC, Pizza Hut

### **🔵 Blue**
- **Psychology:** Trust, calm, professionalism, freshness
- **Effect:** Builds trust, suggests quality
- **Best for:** Seafood, modern brands, professional
- **Examples:** Facebook, Twitter, PayPal

### **🟣 Purple**
- **Psychology:** Luxury, creativity, sophistication
- **Effect:** Premium feel, memorable
- **Best for:** Fine dining, upscale restaurants
- **Examples:** Cadbury, Yahoo, Twitch

## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### **1. Consider Your Logo**
- Match your brand colors to your logo
- If you have a logo, use its colors

### **2. Think About Your Food**
- Warm colors (orange, red) for hot food
- Cool colors (blue, green) for fresh/cold food
- Natural colors (green, brown) for organic

### **3. Consider Your Customers**
- Young customers → Bold, vibrant colors
- Upscale customers → Elegant, muted colors
- Family restaurants → Warm, friendly colors

### **4. Test on Mobile**
- Colors look different on phones
- Test your chosen colors on mobile devices

### **5. Check Dark Mode**
- Your colors auto-adjust for dark mode
- Test both light and dark themes

## 🎨 **Custom Color Combinations**

Want to create your own? Here's the formula:

### **Primary Color (Main Brand)**
- Choose: Orange, Red, Blue, Green, or Purple
- Use for: Main buttons, navigation, highlights

### **Secondary Color (Support)**
- Choose: Complementary color
- Use for: Success messages, secondary actions

### **Accent Color (Alerts)**
- Usually: Red or Orange
- Use for: Delete, cancel, important alerts

### **Good Combinations:**
- Orange + Green (current - excellent!)
- Red + Green (Italian classic)
- Blue + Teal (Modern fresh)
- Purple + Gold (Elegant)
- Green + Orange (Healthy)
